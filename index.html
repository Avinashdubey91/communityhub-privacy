CommunityHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CommunityHub is a private, single-tenant Android app used by one apartment community (Mittal Park View Residency, Kadma, Jamshedpur) to manage maintenance and electricity billing. It is not distributed publicly and has no server component.

What data the app accesses

CommunityHub stores billing, property, and payment records locally on the device in an offline SQLite database. This data never leaves the device except for the two cases below, both of which the user directly controls:

  • Google Drive backup: the app uses Google's drive scope to keep one encrypted backup of the Society's billing records in a single shared Google Drive folder. That folder is shared between the Society's own two collector accounts, which is why the app uses the drive scope rather than the narrower drive.file scope — the narrower scope would hide a folder created by one account from the other. The app does not browse, read, or modify any other file in a user's Drive, and no one outside the Society's own two collector accounts has access to that folder.
  • WhatsApp / email sharing: generated bill and receipt PDFs are shared to WhatsApp or email only when the user explicitly taps a share action.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No account creation or login beyond Google Sign-In for the Drive backup feature.

Data retention and deletion

All data is stored on the user's own device and Google Drive. Uninstalling the app removes the local database. Drive backup files can be deleted directly from Google Drive at any time.
